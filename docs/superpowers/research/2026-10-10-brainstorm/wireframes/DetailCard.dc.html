Detail card: the full reasoning
This holds the information WhoGotLoots puts on its cards, inside the compact table. Only one row is expanded at a time, and the other rows move down.
1
Item header. Facts as chips: slot, armour type, bind, upgrade track and rank, primary stat, socket or tertiary stat.
2
For you. The item it replaces (lowest ring or trinket, or main hand plus off hand for a 2H), the ilvl change, the track change (shown even within one track), the stat change and the Pawn %.
3
Verdict chip. Upgrade, Sidegrade, Downgrade, Can't use or Doesn't want. Sidegrade means the same ilvl with a better score, or the reverse.
4
For the looter. The same comparison against their inspected gear and spec. WhoGotLoots only compares against your gear.
5
Trade estimate. The state, the reason in plain language, the rule used (raid or M+) and how fresh the data is.
6
Actions. Ask (with an optional message edit before sending), link the item, re-inspect, and Trade (enabled only in range and out of combat). Disabled buttons say why.
