Tokens. Click a token to insert it at the cursor. Tokens are filled in when the message is sent.
2
Three templates. Ask (a whisper to the looter), Offer (a whisper to a candidate) and Announce (to the group, raid or instance chat). Each shows a length counter against 255.
3
Live preview. Rendered with a real or test row, including the cross-realm Name-Realm.
4
Guards. One ask per item, and sends queue during encounters and keys. Edit before sending is optional.
5
Auto-ask tab (v1). Off by default. When on, it waits N seconds after the encounter ends and cancels if you ask manually, the looter leaves or the item is traded.
