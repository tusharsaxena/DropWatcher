Tab badge. Counts your drops that are still tradeable. The tab shows only your own loot.
2
For me. The usual verdict. If it is an upgrade, the row asks whether to keep it, and nobody is offered it by default.
3
Trade window. The exact countdown, read from the item. At 10 minutes (configurable) it shows bold and raises a warning.
4
Offer… and Announce. Offer… opens the panel on the right. Announce posts your template to the group or raid.
5
Offer panel. Your drop compared against each member's inspected gear. It works in raids too, where the list scrolls.
6
Status. UPGRADE +n, EQUAL, NO UPGRADE, CAN'T USE, CHECKING… or UNAVAILABLE.
7
Offer and Trade. Offer whispers the offer template. Trade opens the trade window with the item already placed, when in range and out of combat.

Post my tradeables. One line listing everything you can give away.
