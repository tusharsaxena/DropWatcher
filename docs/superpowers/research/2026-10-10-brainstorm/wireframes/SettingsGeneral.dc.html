Settings follow the Ka0s Options layout
1
Pages. The landing page shows the logo, notes and slash commands. Then General, Feed, Comparison, Trade, Messages, Window and Profiles.
2
Header. A "Ka0s X ▸ Page" breadcrumb. The panel cannot be opened in combat.
3
Tabs on every page. One tab per settings group, with a gold underline on the active tab.
4
Master controls. The standard ten controls in their fixed order. Test mode is a checkbox, not a button.
What the other pages hold:
Feed: quality, ilvl floor, gear only, view mode, clear on boss kill, sort.
Comparison: Pawn, weights or none; sidegrade band; compare against owned items; undesired types per spec.
Trade: rule table (raid or M+), countdown warning.
Window: columns, compact preset, row height, toast.
