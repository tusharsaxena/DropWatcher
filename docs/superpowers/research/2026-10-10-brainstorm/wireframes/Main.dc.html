Group drops: every drop, one row each
The layout follows the Loot History window: a 30 px title bar that drags the window, a tab strip with a gold underline on the active tab, a filter bar, the table and a footer. The skin is Ka0s (dark background, 1 px border, gold title). The wireframe is shown in greyscale.
1
Title bar. Drag to move. The window resizes from the corner and Esc closes it.
2
Restriction badge. Shown only during an encounter or an active key. The tooltip says which restriction is active and lists the queued asks.
3
Scan group. Inspects every member out of combat, one at a time.
4
Tabs. A badge counts your tradeable drops. History arrives in v1.
5
Filter bar. Interesting shows upgrades for you and drops the looter can likely trade. All shows everything.
6
Encounter header. Drops are grouped by boss or key run, and each group collapses.
7
Item icon. A real item button: hover shows the tooltip, shift-click links the item and ctrl-click opens dress-up. Clicking the row opens the detail card (02).
8
Their eq. The looter's equipped item. Two icons appear for rings, trinkets and dual wield, with the inspect age beside them.
9
Me and Them. Signed ilvl change with ▲ upgrade, ≈ sidegrade or ▽ downgrade. Colours are inverted for Them. With Pawn on, hovering shows %.
10
Trade. Yes, Likely, Unknown or No, with the reason on hover (03).
11
Ask. One ask per item per looter. The button changes to Queued, then Asked ✓. × dismisses the row.
12
Footer. A quick health check showing inspect freshness, the last loot seen and Pawn status.
