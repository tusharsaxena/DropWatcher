## Title-bar pass (rulings 2, 3 and 4)

The path taken: the library's existing surfaces, not `W.HeaderButtons`. The addon's
`NS.Window.AttachTooltip` gains a `title` placement, which calls `LibKa0s-Tooltip-1.0`'s
`PlaceAttached` on every hover. It falls back to `BOTTOM` on the control's `TOP` + 2, as
`AttachTitleTip` does. The tooltip's title is drawn in `gtip.title`, as it already was. The
title-bar icon button (`NS.WindowTitleBar.IconButton`) now rests in `icon.tint` (`TB.MARK_REST`)
and paints `text.link` (`TB.MARK_HOT`) under the pointer. Its glyph fallback does the same, and
both repaint on a skin switch. Before, these marks rested in `icon.hot` and had no hover color.
These marks never drew a glow, so there is none to keep. Every close is `Core.MakeCloseButton` with
the owner (`NS.MakeCloseButton`), so from the re-copy alone it carries "Close" through
`AttachTitleTip` and paints `close.rest` (which follows `icon.tint`) and `close.hot`.

Cases: `tests/test_title_bar_controls.lua` has two cases per window. The first checks that every
control shows a non-empty tooltip in `gtip.title`, placed by `PlaceAttached`. The second checks
mark rest and hover at `icon.tint` / `text.link`, and close rest and hover at `close.rest` /
`close.hot` with `close.rest` equal to `icon.tint`. Both run under Ka0s Default and Obsidian. A
further case checks that the two skins' tokens differ. All four main-window and Rolls-panel cases
were red before the change.

## Header bands (ruling 5)

`WindowGroup.NewHeader` used to paint its band in `list.box` (Ka0s Default white .06, fainter than a
.08 stripe). It now paints `list.header.bg` (`WG.HEADER_BG`) across the whole row, with its chevron
and text in `list.header.text` (`WG.HEADER_TEXT`). That button is the header on the Drops tab, on
History, on Pending and on every compact-layout header (`modules/CompactGroup.lua` builds its headers
with it). The Rolls panel's item headers paint the same band and chevron. `NS.SKIN_FALLBACK` gains
`list.header.bg` (black .35) and `text.link`, and `tests/test_surface_parity.lua` holds both to the
live library's values. The Insights section headers are the library's Charts and are unchanged here.

Cases: `tests/test_section_header_bands.lua` checks that a group header and a Rolls item header
paint `list.header.bg` under Ka0s Default (black .35) and repaint under Obsidian. It also checks
that the band is not the stripe in either skin, and that compact builds its headers through
`NewHeader`. Both band cases were red before the change.
